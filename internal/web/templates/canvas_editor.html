What you can use

We keep the decoration and remove anything that could run code, fetch from another website, or pretend to be {{.Brand.Name}} asking your visitor for their password. When something goes, we tell you exactly what and why.

Tags

Headings, paragraphs, div, span, lists, blockquote, pre, details, and the whole family of tables, because people built entire layouts out of those and we are not going to stop them.

The nostalgia is deliberate: marquee, blink, font, center, big, tt, nobr, strike, sub, sup, u and marquee inside marquee if you really want.

Styling

Colours, fonts, shadows, borders, radii, gradients, transforms, flexbox, grid, @media, @supports and @keyframes, so animation is on the table.

Your selectors are rewritten to sit beneath #amici-canvas, so your page cannot reach out and restyle the site around it. position: fixed and negative z-index tricks are turned down, because a box that floats over the page is a box that can be made to look like ours.

Links and images

Links are welcome. They are given rel="noopener noreferrer" and made to open in a new tab, so the site you link to never learns whose page sent the visitor.

Images must come from {{.Brand.Name}} itself, or be a small data: image you paste inline. An image hosted elsewhere would tell that host the address of everybody who looks at your page, including people who never agreed to that, so we do not allow it. The stickers below are ours and cost your visitors nothing.

What always goes

Script of any kind, event handlers such as onclick, iframe, object, embed, form and input, anything that loads a font or stylesheet from elsewhere, and meta refresh. Forms and password fields are the important ones: a convincing fake sign-in box on a profile page is the oldest trick there is, and the only reliable defence is that it cannot exist here.
