  • Nobody can find you There is no search, no directory and no "people you may know". Somebody reaches you because they already know your email address, or because you handed them a request code yourself.
  • Codes expire in a day A request code lasts twenty four hours and works once. Nothing you share today becomes a permanent way in.

  • No companies An account is a person. There are no pages, no brands and no advertisers, so there is nothing here that wants your attention for a living.
  • Your page, your markup Write the HTML of your own profile, the way the web used to work. We sanitise it hard and put it in a sandbox, then get out of your way.
  • Nothing to crawl Search engines are turned away at the door, and behind the door every page needs a friendship anyway. There is no version of {{.Brand.Name}} that a stranger can read.

Why bother building this

The large platforms make money when you keep scrolling, and the surest way to keep somebody scrolling is to upset them. That business model has done a great deal of damage, and it is not the only way to put people in touch.

{{.Brand.Name}} is the small version. No ranking, no advertising, no engagement targets, no growth loops, nothing sold about you to anybody. It costs what it costs to run and no more. The longer version is here.
